# Graffiti mobile collection

Eighteen approved ImageGen wallpapers, based on references 1–5, 7–18 and 20; references 6 and 19 duplicate reference 1. Portrait exports are 1080 × 2400 PNGs, with local review files under `output/graffiti-review/`. Each design has a unique title and searchable keywords in `src/assets/graffitiTemplates.js`.

The generation prompt requested faithful source motifs, colors and composition, sharper detail, full-bleed portrait artwork, removal of screenshot margins and interface, and no schedule overlay. The 18 images were uploaded with the scoped Cloudinary manifest and published to the live catalog without replacing existing records.
