# Flowers mobile collection

Forty approved reference-based ImageGen wallpapers, drawn from complete, distinct cards across the supplied screenshots. Portrait exports are 1080 × 2400 PNGs; review originals and source mappings are in `output/flowers-review/`. `src/assets/flowerTemplates.js` holds the individual titles and search keywords.

The scoped Cloudinary uploader published all 40 images, and the revision-checked catalog publisher added the corresponding designs while preserving existing records and publication dates. The collection is mobile-only.
